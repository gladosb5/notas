## Design Context

### Users
Everyone taking notes, including people trying Notas for the first time. The introduction must be fully usable with a keyboard and mouse, with handwriting as an equal alternative.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, clear, welcoming. Help people feel comfortable on an open notebook page.

### Aesthetic Direction
A minimal, quiet introduction, as requested by the creator. Keep guidance on the real paper, using the notebook's typography, theme colours and restrained red accents. Support light and dark themes and reduced motion.

### Design Principles
- Teach through a few real actions on the page.
- Give typing and handwriting equal paths; never require a physical pen.
- Show progress without taking over the notebook.
- Introduce “hey nota” without making AI availability a prerequisite.
- Preserve the user's work and respect skips and replay.

- Nota writes with the user on the paper: short handwritten prompts, no tutorial panel, subtext, progress rail, or oversized headings. Use a muted red for Nota and graphite for examples and actions.
